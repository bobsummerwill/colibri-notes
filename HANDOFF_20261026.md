## Notes, not filed
- **Bank reserve after a failed bank allocation (step 7):** once the bank fails, `v4_reserve_bank_bytes` stays in the
  mirror reserve for the rest of the process (`v4_gpu_mirror_reserve_mb` has no bank-failed check). On the 5070 that
  keeps ~3.4 GB (the 3456 MiB bank) that no bank uses. Its measured effect on decode is nil, so it isn't worth an issue for now.
