# Splitting #1766 + head-followups into smaller PRs (2026-11-03)

## What is being split

`fix/dsv4-head-followups` at `f4586dca` against dev `3b953308`:

- **31 files, +3,086/−162**
- core: `c/deepseek_v4.c` (+775/−125), `c/backend_cuda_dsv4.cu` (+389/−19),
  `docs/deepseek-v4.md` (+189/−7)
- the rest is tests (about 1,300 lines), device-list helpers, loader exports,

The author's commits are already thematic, but they were written on older dev.

`c/deepseek_v4.c`, `docs/deepseek-v4.md`). That is dev drift, not semantic
coupling. The merged branch is the resolved reference; the split should extract
its hunks, not replay raw commits.

## Verdict

**Yes, split it into a 5-PR stack**, with three feature PRs and two small
independent ones. Do not split per commit; the features overlap in
`coli_v4_gpu_engine_open`, the mirror-cache struct/functions and the head/KV
paths.

## What not to do

- Do not split per author commit: 10+ PRs with cross-dependencies and shared
  files is worse than three feature PRs.
- Do not separate tests from their feature; a test PR cannot pass before the
  code it tests.
- Do not put all seven head-followups fixes in one "fixes" PR; distribute them
  to the feature they protect.
- Do not bundle #2405 into #1766; C+ is already a separate PR.
- Do not carry `docs/experiments/` reports into the split; `ca55947c` removed
  them from the branch.

## Recommendation

Land **0** and **S** first, then **1** and **2** in parallel, then **3**, then
the existing C+ PR. Use the final `f4586dca` tree as the reference for hunk
extraction and keep each branch based on current `dev`; do not force a rebase of
the existing #1766 branch. This gives reviewers three feature-sized PRs instead
of one 3,000-line change, without inventing artificial boundaries.
